Pricing is product-specific and depends on the formulation, raw-material specifications, quantity and packaging requirements. We can provide a product-wise quotation upon receiving the final product list/specifications.

Private Label Packing Charges depend on the pack size, packaging material, artwork, printing requirements, and order quantity.
